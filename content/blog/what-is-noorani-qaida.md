Noorani Qaida is a short, step-by-step primer that teaches you to read Arabic as it appears in the Quran. Millions of children and adults around the world begin their Quran journey with it.

## What it teaches

A good Qaida course takes you through, in order:

1. The Arabic letters and their correct sounds
2. How letters change shape when they are joined
3. Short vowels (fathah, kasrah, dammah)
4. Tanween, sukoon and shaddah
5. Long vowels and the madd letters
6. Reading short words, then simple phrases

Each step builds on the one before, so the order matters.

## Why start here

Reading the Quran without this foundation often means guessing sounds and picking up habits that are hard to fix later. Starting with Qaida means the student learns the right pronunciation from the very first lesson, which makes tajweed much easier to learn afterwards.

## Who is it for?

- **Young children** who are starting to read Arabic for the first time
- **Adults and new Muslims** who never had the chance to learn as a child
- **Anyone who reads from habit** and wants to be sure of the basics

## How long does it take?

It depends on age, practice and how often the student has lessons. Many children complete it in a few months with regular one-to-one classes and a little daily practice. Adults often move quicker because they can focus for longer. A teacher will tell you honestly how the student is progressing.

## Tips for learning faster

- Practise for ten minutes every day rather than an hour once a week
- Read out loud so the teacher can hear and correct you
- Revise earlier lessons before starting new ones
